⏰ Daily Routine
9:00 AM pre-market brief · 9:15–9:30 NO trading (opening chaos) · 9:30–10:15 ORB window · 10:15–11:00 watch 200 EMA bounce · 1:00–2:30 avoid new entries (tug-of-war) · 3:25 PM BTST alert · 3:30 close
🎯 The Main Trade
BUY_CALLS: Nifty touches 200 EMA from above + ADX≥18 + RSI<70 → buy the ATM CE shown
BUY_PUTS: Nifty breaks 200 EMA by 0.8% + ADX≥18 → buy the ATM PE shown
WAIT: no trade. Doing nothing is a position.
🛑 Stop Loss (non-negotiable)
1. Index stop from dashboard → exit if hit · 2. Option drops 40% of premium → exit · 3. No move in 2 hours → exit (theta kills) · 4. Warnings fire at 0.6% / 0.3% from stop
📐 Sizing
VIX<12: 2 lots max · VIX 12–22: 1–2 lots · VIX>22: 1 lot or skip. Risk ≤1–2% of capital per trade.
✅ Exit Rules
+50% → trail to breakeven · +100% → take half, trail rest · signal flips to EXIT → exit now · expiry day: exit by 3:15 PM
🚫 Never
Average down · hold through expiry hoping · trade first 15 min · risk >2% · revenge trade after a loss (−5% day = done)
🏦 Smart Money Read
FII🟢+DII🟢 → trust longs · FII🔴+DII🟢 → tug-of-war, tight stops (like today) · FII🔴+DII🔴 → trust shorts · PCR<0.7 → crowd euphoric, contrarian buy PE · PCR>1.4 → panic, contrarian buy CE
🧮 The Math
~60% of signals win. Winners avg +60–100%, losers capped at −40%. 3 winners pay for 2 losers — if you follow the stops every time.
